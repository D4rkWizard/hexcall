What you can record

Line list

All 55 callouts and 12 effects. The easiest way is to record them right in the client's Recording Studio – this list helps you prepare, or if you put finished sound files into the mod folder yourself.

Deutsche Version

Files go into %APPDATA%\Hexcall\mods\<name>\stimme (voice) or …\effekte (effects) – in the client: My Mods → folder icon. Variants: name_2.wav, name_3.wav … – the game picks one at random. Anything missing stays original. The example lines are only suggestions.
