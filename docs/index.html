Hexhub

One client for your games: launch and close them, see what is running and use all mods and tools in one place – the Hexcall sound mod, a 3D garage with stickers, a mod manager, replay statistics and hit analysis for World of Tanks, and Hexrender replay videos for osu!.

⬇ Download for Windows Source on GitHub

What's inside

World of Tanks

  • Sounds (Hexcall) – record your own commander voice and effects, no Wwise needed (guide, line list)
  • 3D garage – every tank in 3D, your own stickers and 3D parts built in as a mod
  • Mods – add, switch off and re-install other people's mods
  • Statistics & replays, hit analysis in 3D, replay → MP4

osu!

  • Replays – all exported and local replays with map, mods, accuracy and background
  • Hexrender – the Hexrender website built right in: pick a replay from your list, render it and get a video link that plays inside Discord
  • Watch replays in osu! with one click

Install

  1. Download the installer Hexhub-Setup-x.y.z.exe (or the ZIP) from the latest release.
  2. Run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway (the program is not signed).
  3. Hexhub finds World of Tanks and osu! by itself – otherwise choose the folders in the settings.
Coming from Hexcall? Your recorded sounds stay in %APPDATA%\Hexcall and appear in the Sounds tab automatically.
