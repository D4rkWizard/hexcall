Hexcall for World of Tanks

Guide

Your own commander voice and your own sound effects (gun, engine, turret …) for World of Tanks – recorded with your microphone, switched on with one click. No Wwise needed, everything runs locally.

1Start

  1. Start Hexcall from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut (without the installer: Hexcall.vbs in the program folder). The client opens in its own window.
  2. The first time you record, the window asks for the microphone: click Allow.
  3. Closing the window stops everything. A background service only runs while the window is open.
  4. WoT is detected automatically. If not: Settings → World of Tanks → Choose folder.
  5. Language: English, German, Russian, Polish, French or Spanish under Settings → Language.

2The areas of the client

Overview

What is active in game right now, your recently edited mods and "Create new sound mod".

My Mods

All sound mods with progress (voice x/55, effects x/12). The active mod is marked green. Record, activate/deactivate, open folder.

Recording Studio

Record step by step. Switch between Voice and Effects at the top.

Active in Game

Choose the slots (mod, original, test tone). Changed slots are marked and the blue Reload button lights up – it builds the sounds. Mod off switches everything to original, Turn back on restores the previous state.

Settings

Language, theme (DarkWizard, Commander, Light), WoT folder, updates (automatic if you like), animations.

Help

Quick guide and a small changelog. Bottom left: WoT status, Start WoT and – while WoT runs – your player name.

3Recording studio

  • Every callout with its situation and an example line. The list is on the left, a tick marks finished steps.
  • Red button or Space: start and stop recording, with a live meter. The take is played back right away.
  • Skip: the callout stays original in game.
  • Save as an additional variant: several versions per callout, the game picks one at random.
  • Drag & drop: simply drop WAV, MP3, M4A or OGG files into the window.
  • After the last callout the studio asks whether you want to continue with the effects.
  • Done – activate in game builds the mod and switches it on.

All callouts and effects are listed in the line list.

4Two voices: Standard and Commander

WoT setting (Sound › voice-over)What you hear
StandardVoice · Standard (test tone: two beeps)
CommanderVoice · Commander (test tone: three beeps)

With "Commander" WoT uses the voice of your commander's nation. Special commanders (Battle Pass, bloggers, events) have a voice of their own – with them you keep hearing their original. Effects always apply. WoT loads its sounds when the game starts: if the game is running when you press Reload, Hexcall offers Apply & restart WoT.

5Testing

  1. Under Active in Game set everything to Test tone (beep) and press Reload.
  2. Start (or restart) WoT and fire a few shots and drive around in the training room.
  3. Instead of voice, gun and engine you hear beeps – two with "Standard", three with "Commander". Then everything works.

6Tips for good recordings

Voice

Keep it short: 0.5 to 2 seconds. Clear, and loud when it fits – it should sound like battle.

Loops

Engine, turret, tracks and fire play in a loop: 2–4 seconds of steady sound without a start or end. The game changes the engine pitch itself.

Bangs

Gun and explosions: a short, punchy bang.

Automatic

Silence and clicks at the start and end are removed, the volume is levelled and everything is built into the game at 48 kHz.

7Updates

Hexcall: on start the client checks for a new version and warns you about an outdated one with Update now. If you like, it installs updates automatically. Your mods are kept.

World of Tanks: the sounds live in res_mods\<version> for each game version. After a WoT update Hexcall shows a notice with Turn sounds back on – this builds your setup for the new version.

9Limits

  • Special crew voices (bloggers, events, Sabaton etc.) are not replaced.
  • Engine sounds cover the 14 engine types the game currently uses.
  • Reload sounds and hits on your own tank are not included (yet).
